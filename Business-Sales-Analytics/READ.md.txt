# Business Sales Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes business sales data to identify revenue trends, top-performing products, profitable categories, and regional performance.

## Tools Used

* Microsoft Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Dashboard Design

## Key KPIs

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Sold

## Analysis Performed

* Sales Trend Analysis
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Category
* Profit by Region
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Discount vs Profit Analysis
* Top 5 Customers Analysis

## Key Insights

* Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
* West region was the strongest-performing region.
* High discounts reduced profitability.
* A small number of customers contributed a significant share of revenue.

## Recommendations

* Focus on high-profit categories.
* Improve performance in weaker regions.
* Optimize discount strategies.
* Strengthen relationships with top customers.
