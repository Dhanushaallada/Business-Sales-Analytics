BUSINESS SALES PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

KEY INSIGHTS

1. Sales by Category

* Technology generated the highest sales revenue (₹839,893).
* Furniture generated sales of ₹754,748.
* Office Supplies generated sales of ₹731,893.

Recommendation:
Focus marketing and inventory planning on Technology products to maximize revenue growth.

2. Profit by Category

* Technology generated the highest profit (₹146,543).
* Office Supplies generated a profit of ₹126,023.
* Furniture generated the lowest profit (₹19,730).

Recommendation:
Increase focus on Technology products and review the profitability of Furniture products.

3. Sales by Region

* West region generated the highest sales (₹739,814).
* East region generated sales of ₹691,828.
* South region generated the lowest sales (₹391,722).

Recommendation:
Investigate opportunities to improve sales performance in the South region.

4. Profit by Region

* West region generated the highest profit (₹110,799).
* East region generated a profit of ₹94,883.
* Central region generated the lowest profit (₹39,865).

Recommendation:
Apply successful sales strategies from the West region to lower-performing regions.

5. Top Selling Products

* Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest revenue-generating product.
* A small number of products contributed a large share of total revenue.

Recommendation:
Prioritize high-performing products in promotions and inventory management.

6. Discount vs Profit Analysis

* Higher discounts (30%, 40%, 50%, 70%, and 80%) resulted in negative profits.
* Excessive discounting reduced overall profitability.

Recommendation:
Implement a controlled discount strategy to protect profit margins.

7. Top Customers Analysis

* Sean Miller was the highest revenue-generating customer (₹25,043).
* Tamara Chand and Raymond Buch were also among the top customers.

Recommendation:
Develop loyalty programs and personalized offers for high-value customers.

CONCLUSION

Technology products and the West region are the strongest contributors to business performance. The company should focus on profitable products, strengthen customer retention, and optimize discount policies to improve long-term growth and profitability.
